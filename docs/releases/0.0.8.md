## Changes

- Anti-fingerprinting now uses WebKit's own protection, the one Safari uses for Advanced Fingerprinting Protection. Canvas and audio readouts get per-session noise and the screen reports its full size, while the page's functions stay WebKit's own, so bot checks that look for replaced functions no longer refuse Barc. GoDaddy's Microsoft 365 sign-in passed every run with it. Where a macOS release lacks the switch, Barc falls back to its script, and the floating switch on sites known to refuse it.
- **⌥Q** copies a screenshot of the full page to the clipboard and **⌥W** copies the visible area. They replace ⌃⇧⌘4 and ⌃⇧⌘3, and work while typing in a field or on a page. Change them under Settings → Shortcuts.
- The performance gauge moved from beside Home at the bottom of the sidebar to the top, straight after the window controls. Below about 241 points of sidebar width, back, forward and reload take their own row beneath it.
